# Catstagram

Catstagram is a Virtual Adoption App, built for people who want to share and post about their pets and help them find a loving family through an adoption center. The app allows users to browse posts about adoptable cats, add their own posts, and connect with potential adopters to provide a safe, loving home.
## Features
- Post Creation: Users can share posts about cats available for adoption with descriptions and photos.
- Adoption Center: A feature that allows users to search for adoptable cats and apply for adoption.
- Profile Management: Users can manage their profile, including their pet details, adoption status, and contact information.
- Likes & Comments: Users can like and comment on posts to show their interest or ask questions.
- Notifications: Get notified when someone shows interest in a post or when a pet is adopted

## Technologies Used
- FlutterFlow: A visual development platform to build and manage the app.
- Firebase: For authentication, Firestore database, and real-time updates.
- Firebase Authentication: Manages user login and registration.
